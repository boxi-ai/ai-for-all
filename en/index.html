An AI literacy course for everyone: 8 shared core modules, with tracks for children and schools, university students of any major, working adults, older adults and teachers. Each module has notes, a 3–5 minute video and an interactive web page. The signature module is C4 Checking: AI makes mistakes, so learn to check.

8 SHARED CORE MODULES
WHO IT'S FOR · TRACKS

Children / K–12

School lessons and after-school clubs; mostly unplugged activities, with the teacher at the keyboard and the children as detectives.

University, any major

16 weeks, 2 credits: checking logs, an AI-off midterm, an AI + your-subject project and an oral defence.

Working adults

A micro-credential of 8 × 90-minute sessions. Bring a real task from your own job.

Older adults

6 in-person sessions with a family member or volunteer alongside, with a focus on avoiding scams.

Teachers

A 30-hour course: how to teach checking, and how to assess it.

Accessibility

Screen-reader friendly, Chinese and English subtitles, large print, audio version and an offline pack.

Every fact in the notes links to its source; examples marked “Demo” were made up for teaching. The English edition is adapted, not translated word for word: examples specific to China are kept and paired with an international equivalent.
